# Our Monthsary Map

A 3D toy map of our places, from España Blvd to Quezon City, Mandaluyong, Greenbelt and Makati, Rockwell, MOA, Baclaran, Bacoor, Dasmariñas and Tagaytay. Tap a place and a little car drives there.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It loads three.js from a CDN, so it needs an internet connection.

- **Tap a pin or a place name** to drive there.
- **Ulan** makes it rain and floods España.
- **Play lofi** starts an original lofi loop generated in the browser.
- **Whole map** zooms out to show everything; **España** brings you back.
